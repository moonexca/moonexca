# Soledad Yash

Data analytics and applied machine learning in Dublin, Ireland.

I build interpretable, decision-oriented analyses across governance, retail, agriculture, and innovation. My work emphasizes reproducibility, leakage prevention, temporal validation, meaningful baselines, and honest limitations.

## Selected projects

- [Wheat Production Analytics](https://github.com/moonexca/wheat-production-analytics-capstone/pull/1) — Leakage-safe cross-validation; the median baseline reached RMSE 1.6999 and outperformed the learned models on the available data.
- [Online Retail Recommendation & Market Basket](https://github.com/moonexca/online-retail-recommendation-market-basket/pull/1) — Last-invoice holdout across 3,400 users; truncated SVD improved Hit Rate@10 from 0.231 to 0.322 and catalogue coverage from 2.03% to 22.75%.
- [Ireland Innovation Ecosystem](https://github.com/moonexca/ireland-innovation-ecosystem-analytics/pull/1) — A 2021–2024 forward holdout exposes target leakage and benchmarks trend models against last observation.
- [Governance Effectiveness & Global Society](https://github.com/moonexca/governance-effectiveness-global-society-ml/pull/1) — Clustering and baseline-aware forecasting; the naive forecast beat ARIMA on the saved benchmark.
